Methodology

Evidence classes

Every claim carries [F] fact, [I] inference, [H] hypothesis or [N] negative, rendered as an inline marker.

Facts, not blobs

No proprietary payload is ever committed. Only hashes, sizes, offsets, counts and short hex samples.

Coverage states

Catalogued: per-file rows exist. Count-known: an upstream count exists but no per-file layer. Inventory-only: neither.

Leverage

Ranked by device reach, not row count.
